# Build audit — 25 November 2026

**Status: experimental prototype, not a finished game or a release candidate.** This project does not meet the master bible's final-game acceptance criteria.

## Comparison with the brief

- Opening: extruded 3D studio and title text, a dimensional ring emblem and a timed 3D workshop prologue exist. Their visual quality and cinematic impact have not been assessed in a rendered run. This is not a finished CAD-quality logo or a finished story cinematic.
- Phone: the call is now answered automatically, and the phone screen identifies Eli. The pickup combines stock motion with arm pose keys and a hand-relative phone attachment; the 3D shot continues during the voiced call. It is still provisional: hand contact, framing, phone-to-ear placement and believability are not visually verified; facial performance and lip sync are not implemented.
- Character and city: temporary stock rigged characters, imported props, open-front storefronts, warehouse checkpoint and a distant harbor. These are not final character or world art; storefronts lack collision and the harbor is not navigable.
- Audio: the call, Tessa's lines, witness encounter and collector confrontation have AI-synthesized voices, licensed stock music and synthetic effects. Descriptions and incidental text remain unvoiced; the mix and performances have not been reviewed in-engine or on iPhone.
- Play: the short prototype continues from the witness to a 3D van and a collector encounter with a confirmed bargain, withdrawal, or basic timed combat. The saved outcome changes Tessa's immediate response. Fulfilling the bargain, later Sade consequences, driving and a 60-minute campaign are not implemented. The named dialogue and characters are proposed, not established canon.
- Mobile: touch controls are preliminary, not validated for safe areas or accessibility. Xogot, iPhone 16 and iPhone 13 mini operation, performance and compatibility have not been tested.

## Verification

Godot 4.7.2 Linux headless can import the project and run the automated smoke script (`godot --headless --path . --script res://tests/smoke.gd`). The tests cover scene construction, extruded mesh creation, timing and skipping, phone scene transition, character animation clips, witness interaction, and movement after the slice. They do not render pixels or test a real phone. Headless uses a dummy display driver, not a graphical rendering check: https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_displayserver.html

Research references and original creative proposals are in the repository's `docs/LEVERAGE_worldwide_creative_research.md`. Relevant technical guidance: Godot's TextMesh creates 3D text geometry (https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/3d/3d_text.html); Godot recommends measuring 3D performance on target hardware (https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/performance/optimizing_3d_performance.html). Neither proves this prototype meets the visual or performance brief.

Research: [Godot input event flow](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/inputs/inputevent.html), [indexed screen touch](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_inputeventscreentouch.html), [analog vectors](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.0/classes/class_input.html), [display safe area](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_displayserver.html); Xbox accessibility guidelines on [objectives](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/109), [UI navigation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/112), [contrast](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/102), and [text](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/101). Applied semantic analog actions, finger-index ownership and explicit objective markers; these sources are design guidance, not certification.

Keyboard WASD/arrows, Q/E, J/K, Enter/Space, X, M; touch uses a left analog stick, buttons and right-side drag. Dialogue buttons receive controller focus. Headless tests exercise indexed touch and action setup; a physical Xbox controller, GUI focus traversal and real multitouch have not been tested. No remapping, sensitivity options, vertical camera, vibration or full console parity yet.
- **Navigation:** North-up schematic of the currently playable block with position, heading, named stage target, distance and a straight-line bearing. The line does not route around obstacles; the diagram is not a surveyed street map or full city. No target beyond the current prototype endpoint. Stage transitions and marker state need device visual review.
- **Save:** Two alternated checksummed slots, validation and fallback to legacy v1/v2 saves. Headless exercises round-trip, damaged-slot recovery and invalid outcomes; no claim of power-loss-safe filesystem atomicity or iOS persistence verification.
- **Opening/characters/world:** previous warehouse/phone/cinematic changes and licensed interim assets remain. Marcus visibility and phone readability from the earlier device screenshots, contact with the ear, clothing and environment quality remain unverified on a real renderer. Stock character, no authored facial animation/lip sync, simplified district and no full city are not final assets.
- **Story/campaign/combat/audio:** a short branching prototype, not the planned 60-minute campaign; most missions, systems, final performances, nuanced combat and full audio mix are still to be produced. Existing generated voices and licensed interim score need listening and editorial review. No complete balance or accessibility validation.
- **Device/technical QA:** fresh headless imports and logic tests cannot prove graphics, sound, input mapping, safe areas, battery, thermals or frame rate. Test on iPhone 13/16 and Xogot, including notch/home-indicator safe area, controller mapping and long sessions; profile before setting supported performance targets. GUI uses fixed 1280×720 logical coordinates, not full safe-area adaptation. Scope remains work in progress, not an exhaustive audit or final release.

## Grounded realism direction and first physical-material pass (2026-11-25)

Owner clarified the generated harbor image is the desired *actual game look and feel*: grounded people, realistic materials, night lighting, wet surfaces and atmosphere, not just title art. Asset size is not a reason to simplify; retain source detail unless measurement and a specific compatibility/performance need justify an alternative. The image is a visual target, not proof that the current game reaches it.

Assets and licences are credited in `assets/materials/README.md`; files are unchanged from the selected 2K mirror and were not downscaled here. More detailed models, properly authored surfaces/UV scale, lighting, real people, animation and atmospheric effects are still required to approach the image. These materials remain unreviewed on a rendered screen and iPhone. Existing 512px prop assets are legacy interim assets, not a newly approved quality ceiling.

Added optional vibration on incoming-phone buzz and combat impacts, with an on/off control in the title menu; it requests handheld haptics and connected controller rumble through Godot's Input API. Device settings, iOS version and Xogot may affect behavior, so this is unverified on actual hardware; no claim of matching the visual/audio pulse until tested. Reference: https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_input.html. Surface implementation follows https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/3d/standard_material_3d.html. The user-reported missing pickup/voices and hang remain open; headless loading cannot establish the cause. Visual fidelity, sound, haptics, safe areas and device performance all require device testing. Campaign and full-world production remain unfinished.

## Grounded in-engine environment pass (2026-11-25)

The owner requested that the game itself—not an image or title backdrop—look realistic. This increment replaces the ringing close-up's floating box tabletop with a textured, real-scale CC0 wooden table model, resizes the phone geometry to approximately 18 cm, uses the same table asset in the pickup shot, applies 2K CC0 asphalt maps to the street, and assembles a small 2K-textured modular factory facade in the 3D district. Original selected mirror files are retained without reducing texture resolution; see `assets/realistic/README.md` and `assets/materials/README.md` for authors, license and source revisions. The backdrop is not navigable. The darker, reduced-roughness asphalt is an approximation, not a simulated rain/water reflection system.

A fresh Godot 4.7.2 headless import and logic checks exercise table and facade resource instantiation and sequence progression; they cannot assess actual appearance or iPhone memory/performance. **The game does not yet match the reference image:** characters are still interim stock rigs, most architecture/harbor remains simple geometry, and rain, image-based reflections, authored cinematic acting, final environmental lighting and device validation remain unfinished. The user-reported call hang, missing pickup visibility and missing audio are still unconfirmed and require diagnosis on Xogot; these changes do not claim to resolve them. ## Movement and camera collision pass (2026-11-25)

Player movement now uses Godot's CharacterBody3D and physics-time move_and_slide rather than directly changing position. Invisible collision shapes approximate the warehouse side walls, shop back/side walls and van; the camera ray shortens its offset where these colliders intervene. The van collider is only active when the van is visible. Headless physics checks cover the warehouse wall and camera obstruction, but this is not full-world collision: floors are flat, furnishings, storefront posts, characters, most props and the harbor do not all have collision; no navigation mesh, pathfinding, jumping, driving, crowd physics or device playtest is claimed. Sources: https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_characterbody3d.html and https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_physicsdirectspacestate3d.html.

The owner's requested release-readiness remains unmet: real iPhone/Xogot rendering and voice playback are still unverified, the reported missing people/phone pickup and silent call are not conclusively diagnosed, the opening and combat animation remain temporary, and the world/campaign is far short of the requested vision. This increment is a step, not an assertion of completion. See earlier entries for research, asset provenance and device limitations.

The answer sequence also has a 135-second watchdog (longer than its maximum bounded dialogue timing) that can recover to the first checkpoint if its callback is stranded while the scene tree is still running. It cancels pending answer callbacks and invalidates stale recovery timers; headless tests exercise simulated recovery and cancellation. This is a fail-safe, not a diagnosis or fix for a hard device freeze, missing rendering, or inaudible voice, and can skip unheard dialogue in a failure case.

## Control mandate and combat prototype (2026-11-25)

Supersedes the earlier A-strike/B-guard/Y-map description. Controller and on-screen combat controls are now X punch, Y heavy, A kick and B dodge; B interacts outside combat. The controller View button or M opens the map, leaving Y available in combat. Keyboard J/L/I/K correspond to punch/heavy/kick/dodge. Dodge applies a short movement impulse and temporary avoidance window; attacks have different windups/cooldowns, range and damage, with hit resolution checking current distance and facing. Dodging cancels a pending attack. Independent camera input remains separate from walking. Headless checks cover mappings, touch-button logical rectangles, dodge timing, facing, windup and encounter persistence. Sources: https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/inputs/inputevent.html and https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/107.

These are provisional mechanics: the existing stock model still uses substitute gestures instead of authored punch, heavy, kick, dodge and hit animations; attack sound plays at initiation, not contact, and combat still lacks robust enemy AI, obstacle-aware enemies, weapon states and balanced encounters. Touch sizes/spacing are logical coordinates, not verified physical-size or safe-area compliance. Controller, multi-touch, animations, responsiveness, collision and dodge feel have not been tested on iPhone/Xogot. No performance profile, device optimization, finalized architecture or release readiness is claimed. Physical-device evidence remains required.
