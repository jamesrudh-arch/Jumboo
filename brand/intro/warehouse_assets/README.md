# Warehouse asset preparation — not a playable scene

`portable_generator.glb` is a repackaged 1K-texture version of [Portable Generator](https://polyhaven.com/a/portable_generator), by James Ray Cock. Poly Haven's [license](https://polyhaven.com/license) dedicates its models to CC0, including commercial use and redistribution. The actual inputs were obtained from the unaffiliated [zuujis-cc0 mirror](https://github.com/zuujis-cc0/polyhaven-models-industrial-and-infrastructure) at commit `5be6c9fa483503256bb2d417f07b1a22e519f2df`. The preparation script checks the mirror's SHA-256 manifest values for all six inputs. These files have not been independently compared to Poly Haven's direct downloads. No third-party promotional images are included.

The glTF and dependencies were imported and exported as a self-contained GLB by Blender 4.2; no deliberate geometry simplification was applied. Output SHA-256: `32c70a4de5548257df745939b638bb4a5f2dcaa098b206647232113854d02bd1`. Export logged multiple image sampler warnings. Comparing the source and exported re-import in Blender gave 4 meshes, 26,419 triangles, and world bounding dimensions approximately 0.818 × 0.564 × 0.577 m for both. Individual Cycles renders were inspected and preserved the model's visible textures in this view. A Godot 4.4.1 headless template parsed the raw GLB and generated a scene, but reported missing renderer and script cache. This does not verify material appearance, collision, gameplay, lighting, memory, iPhone performance, or Xogot behavior. The asset is **not yet used by the game** and is not a completed warehouse or a placeholder scene.

Rebuild: obtain the pinned repository above, then run `blender --background --python tools/prepare_warehouse_generator.py -- /path/to/portable_generator/1k/portable_generator_1k.gltf`. Standalone `bpy` 4.2 was used here with display stubs and `BYPASS_BPY_SHUTDOWN=1`; its output need not be byte-identical across exporter versions.

## Other assets evaluated

[Security Camera 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/security_camera_01) (Alexander Otterbeck, Yann Kervran) and [Overhead Crane](https://polyhaven.com/a/overhead_crane) (Timothy3D) were retrieved in 1K glTF form from the same mirror. Blender imports measured 13,571 and 89,964 triangles respectively, and bounds of 0.170 × 0.554 × 0.286 m and 12.492 × 4.000 × 5.133 m respectively. Test renders showed recognisable physical models, but the very bright test lighting washed out surface detail. Neither was integrated: the crane in particular needs scene-specific placement, support structure and mobile profiling. The earlier Kenney City Builder assets were also inspected but their toy-like appearance did not fit the requested direction; they were not adopted. Asset rights, usability and inspection are not proof of a finished environment.
