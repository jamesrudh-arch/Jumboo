## Pickup contact diagnostic — failed, not presented for approval

A separate Blender-only forward-kinematics experiment used the approved Marcus source and phone study to test a reach from the bench and raise to chest height. The actual rendered checkpoints are `docs/previews/intro/pickup_contact_diagnostic.png` and `pickup_hold_diagnostic.png`; `tools/render_pickup_contact_diagnostic.py` reproduces them with bpy 4.2. The approved source model and phone scene are unchanged. The blank background and slab are diagnostic staging only, not a proposed game setting. No audio or game scene was produced.

**This attempt fails the pickup quality gate:** at contact the hand presses onto the display instead of clearly gripping the sides; in the raised pose the fingers and thumb do not convincingly wrap the phone, the free arm remains in an A-pose, and there is no head/eye or conversational performance. Positioning the phone relative to the hand during the lift does not prove contact or collision-free movement between samples. This must not be integrated or treated as an approved pickup. The separately approved vibration, ringtone and dialogue are not connected here.

Inspection of the Blender-imported original also found that the right upper-arm and forearm bones have very long displayed tails (roughly 26.6 m and 25.7 m after the armature's 0.01 scale), despite joint heads being close together. This is an observation of this Blender import, not proof of a Godot rig problem. The test used rotations rather than relying on those tails for IK. A workable pickup needs a validated control rig/IK approach based on joint positions, finger poses around the prop and contact/clipping checks throughout the movement, without changing the approved source appearance.

## Finger-side contact experiment — still not ready

A second offline study rotates the wrist, bends the fingers and repositions the phone so the hand approaches its long edges. `tools/render_pickup_grip_diagnostic.py` rebuilds close-up frames `docs/previews/intro/pickup_grip_contact.png` and `pickup_grip_hold.png` plus `pickup_grip_metrics.json`. This is a diagnostic, **not an approved performance or integrated scene**. The original approved phone and Marcus source were not changed.

The coarse test transforms vertices with more than 50% weight from each named finger group into the phone's local frame and compares them with its rectangular bounding box at frames 22 and 40. At each sampled pose, 135 thumb and 40 index vertices fall inside that box; the nearest sampled middle, ring and pinky vertices are about 15, 40 and 61 mm from it. This identifies substantial penetration and gaps, not a satisfactory grip. The box is an approximation, the test does not detect all triangle intersections or check the continuous motion, and the target hand pose and free arm still need work. Do not interpret the numbers as full collision QA. A visually and technically convincing pickup remains unfinished.

## Provisional mouth-motion test

The owner correctly noted that Marcus's mouth did not move in the preceding conversation preview. `docs/previews/intro/mouth_motion_study.mp4` tests a separate correction over the full approved dialogue: a limited lower-face deformation and a small dark mouth-interior overlay are driven by the amplitude of Marcus's speech. The recorded brother-only intervals are gated closed at the sampled animation times. This is an **approximate audio-reactive open/close test**, not phoneme/viseme lip-sync, facial capture, or a production facial rig; the overlay is not an actual modeled oral cavity and may show artifacts. Marcus's expression otherwise stays largely fixed, and the 8 fps, 320×320 render is a low-resolution offline preview. The approved Marcus GLB and previous conversation study are unchanged. The phone pickup, connected-call state, character emotion, reliable mouth anatomy and smooth, accurate speech animation remain unfinished.

`tools/preview_marcus_mouth_motion.py` creates a separate `mouth_motion_study.blend`, the MP4 and `mouth_motion_audit.json`. It adds a simple shape key and a head-weighted overlay to a copy of the study; it does not modify the source mesh. The script gates against the durations of the approved individual speaker clips and samples audio RMS at 8 fps; it does not infer phonemes or guarantee exact speech boundaries between samples. The sample audit reports zero nonzero jaw values outside Marcus's labelled intervals; this is a structural check, not an evaluation of perceived synchronization. The MP4 decoded locally in full. Rebuilding requires Blender Python 4.2, NumPy and imageio-ffmpeg; no Godot/Xogot or device test is implied.
