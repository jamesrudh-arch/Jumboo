# LEVERAGE: CINDER CITY — Cast, Dialogue and Continuity Workbook

**Draft 0.4 · 25 November 2026.** New canon is proposed, not historically recovered or owner-approved. The supplied brief's original names and precise Chapter One ending details are not specified. Read with the three existing production-design documents. This workbook advances the design; it does not claim the full production bible, implementation, licensing or device testing is complete.

## 1. Narrative truth and player knowledge

### Proposed factual sequence

- Keira Moss, an auditor, builds a partial reconciliation index after noticing that a shipping authorisation and payment references do not agree. She knows there are irregularities; she does not know who ordered an abduction.
- Eli Vale signs a routine correction to a dispatch record before it is altered. He later finds that the revision associates an innocent driver, Omar Saye, with a suspicious transfer. Eli copies a partial index and leaves an ordinary maintenance tag containing a lookup fragment. The fragment identifies a record, not the location of the missing man.
- Victor Rusk, Meridian security chief, exploits credentials to redirect a shipment. Celia Ardent chooses corporate containment after learning of irregularities; she did not order Eli's kidnapping. Pavel sells a disputed obligation, not ownership of a person.
- Darius Cole's crew takes Eli intending to recover a copy and hold him temporarily. Rusk's people subsequently move Eli without Darius's authorisation. Eli continues acting to protect Omar and the witness, rather than existing only as a rescue target.
- Tessa Ward saw a collector's vehicle and withheld the sighting to protect an employee's indebted family. She did not arrange the abduction. Marcus uncovers the omission through independent records and Omar's vehicle identification.
- Marcus can use or disclose partial evidence, collect independent corroboration, negotiate for Eli, and decide how to transfer the records. No single entry is proof of the entire conspiracy.

### Scene-knowledge safeguards

Every dialogue condition below means **knowledge explicitly obtained**, not physical proximity to evidence. Sender, recipient and scope are separate; a recipient of transaction IDs does not automatically know a witness's name. A complete design validator needs fixtures for answered/missed calls, each M01 witness choice, both Ledger paths, each evidence recovery route and Tessa's trust states. For every dialogue node, test the negative case: the line must not appear when its prerequisite knowledge is false.

## 2. Character dossiers and voice direction

All ages, relationships and names below are **proposed**. Wardrobe and performance are creative briefs; no asset or casting is sourced.

### Marcus Vale · protagonist · 33–38
- **Silhouette:** broad mechanic's jacket, work trousers and practical shoes; stance tends toward stillness until he commits. Recognisable without a heroic costume.
- **Public/private:** capable and guarded; privately considers leaving the city and feels guilty about wanting distance from family obligations.
- **Want/fear:** get Eli home; fear that protecting family will make him behave like the people exploiting it. **Leverage held:** local trust, repair knowledge, evidence. **Leverage over him:** family, shop and the knowledge he has concealed his plan to leave.
- **Arc:** learns that honesty is not the same as control; can protect or compromise others through his evidence choices. No fixed moral score.
- **Voice/performance:** concise, uses dry humour when uncomfortable; at pressure he asks short concrete questions rather than speeches. Slow, grounded idle; committed but imperfect fighting. Requires phone handling, varied listening, injury and dialogue reactions.
- **Knowledge:** starts knowing Eli is troubled, not the abduction chain; discovers source-specific leads; knows Rusk's role only once corroborated. Outcome: Eli's return and Marcus's future depend on authored choices, not automatic reconciliation.

### Eli Vale · brother · 28–34
- **Silhouette:** lighter work jacket, security pass and messenger bag; posture attentive and alert. Logistics clerk, not helpless cargo.
- **Want/fear:** protect Omar and the witness; fears his signature makes him responsible. Holds the lookup fragment and knowledge of his own actions; others hold his past signature and custody over him.
- **Arc/knowledge:** begins with partial index and suspicion, not full proof; deliberately leaves a fragment before capture, later confirms the correction was his. His release is a shared operation.
- **Voice/performance:** quick, teasing and indirect when afraid; humour stops abruptly under pressure. Facial shift from ease to alarm, phone and restrained escape gestures. Outcomes: alive in successful main route; trust and testimony willingness respond to the player's conduct.

### Ruth Vale · mother · 55–65
- **Silhouette:** food-counter apron over durable clothes; economical movements, direct eye contact. Wants both sons safe; fears family silence repeats a past loss. Holds knowledge of an old obligation but not Ledger contents until told.
- **Voice:** direct, warm without sentimentality; uses practical questions as a way to show concern. Staging uses work at the counter rather than perpetual exposition. In aftermath she challenges Marcus's decision without dictating it.

### Nia Vale · cousin and nurse · 29–39
- **Silhouette:** practical layered clothes and medical bag outside work; purposeful gait. Wants civilians protected; fears Marcus's tactics will endanger patients. Has medical contacts, not a guarantee of immunity.
- **Voice:** measured, quick under pressure; avoids treating suffering as a plot device. Learns about injuries only via observed contact or a call. Can assist an injured witness if reachable; otherwise clinic route remains.

### Tessa Ward · friend and shop partner · 32–40
- **Silhouette:** work coveralls, short utility jacket; distinctive upright stance. Wants her employees safe and garage open; fears their family's debt will be used against them. Holds transport, shop access and knowledge of the collector's vehicle.
- **Arc:** withholding a relevant sighting damages trust; she may support Marcus, set boundaries or stay away based on the confrontation. She did not arrange the kidnapping.
- **Voice:** blunt, practical, affectionate sarcasm; defensive when confronted, eventually precise about what she knew and when. Requires shop interactions, vehicle hand-off and multiple listening reactions.

### Omar Saye · port driver · 30–45
- **Silhouette:** reflective workwear and a worn cap, cautious walk. Wants unpaid wages and protection for family; fears being blamed for a route he did not drive. Holds a signed route copy and can identify a repair.
- **Voice:** patient with mundane details; stops talking when a question would expose family. He knows the allocated vehicle, not the kidnapper's identity. Can provide a delivery window if trusted; shift schedule remains an alternative.

### Lena Ortiz · reporter · 30–45
- **Silhouette:** weatherproof coat, shoulder bag and phone recorder; brisk, observant movement. Wants evidence that can withstand scrutiny; fears exposing sources. Holds a publication channel and independent storage.
- **Voice:** asks for provenance and exact language rather than sensational accusations. Disclosure path gives her only what Marcus chooses to share; she cannot identify Sade without an explicit event. She may hold publication pending corroboration.

### Darius Cole · local crew leader · 35–45
- **Silhouette:** dark layered streetwear, immobile shoulders; watches exits before entering a room. Wants independence from Meridian influence; fears his crew's debts will make them disposable. Holds local safe passage and knowledge of the first interception.
- **Voice:** controlled, rarely raises volume; threats are precise and accompanied by a plausible way out. Arc: can provide assistance or remain hostile based on promises; cannot supply information about Rusk's second transfer until told or shown proof.

### Mina Cho · club manager and broker · 35–50
- **Silhouette:** sharply cut jacket with practical footwear; calm, upright posture. Wants staff protected; fears clients treating her premises as neutral territory for violence. Knows observed meetings, not their secret content; controls a back-room route.
- **Voice:** tactful and economical; politely identifies limits. She asks Sade for consent before offering a hiding route. Her help depends on safety and prior conduct, with another route available.

### Sade Okoro · market organiser and witness · 30–45
- **Silhouette:** layered rainwear, carrier bag and distinct patterned scarf; checks the street while working. Wants neighbours safe; fears becoming an identifiable source. Holds a description of a damaged van, not its occupants.
- **Voice:** clear, guarded; refuses to speculate. A reassurance promise can be kept or broken through explicit disclosure. Pressure changes trust but never fabricates additional knowledge. Animation needs work with crates, eye-line changes and a grounded exit.

### Inspector Mara Voss · financial-crime detective · 40–55
- **Silhouette:** plain coat and visible notebook, deliberate gait. Wants a case that can survive scrutiny; fears a leak within her unit. Holds access to verification and limited institutional resources, not a guarantee the police are uniformly honest.
- **Voice:** restrained, separates facts from allegations. Learns a record only if sent it or obtains it in an authored investigation. Outcomes vary with admissibility and disclosure, not an instant city-wide cleanup.

### Officer Ishan Rao · patrol officer · 25–35
- **Silhouette:** uniform with practical rain layer; posture tense when checking messages. Wants his indebted sister protected; fears professional and family consequences. Knows dispatch information available to his role.
- **Voice:** polite, hesitant before committing to a lie. Can supply a warning or mislead under pressure, with evidence of his circumstances discoverable but not assumed. No instant access to every case record.

### Celia Ardent · Meridian executive · 45–60
- **Silhouette:** structured coat, minimal jewellery, careful stance; corporate authority without an exaggerated villain costume. Wants to contain a scandal and preserve the company; fears being made sole scapegoat. Holds contracts, access and authority over some staff.
- **Arc:** chose containment, did not order the kidnapping; may keep or break a narrowly specified exchange promise depending on authored conditions. Her bargaining route provides an Eli lead, not proof of trustworthiness.
- **Voice:** exact, qualified, never casually confesses facts she does not know. When challenged with corroboration she acknowledges her own decision without accepting unsupported accusations.

### Victor Rusk · Meridian security chief · 40–55
- **Silhouette:** functional security attire, clipped movement, keeps distance. Wants control of evidence; fears exposure of unauthorised conduct. Holds security access and an altered authorisation trail.
- **Voice:** calm commands and short operational language; confidence weakens only with credible evidence or loss of authority. He learns the disclosure only through an authored leak; no omniscient pursuit. Negotiation can alter the confrontation without erasing accountability.

### Pavel Ilyin · debt intermediary · 45–60
- **Silhouette:** understated tailoring and a worn ledger wallet; hands remain visible. Wants obligations honoured; fears losing his market of trust. Holds transaction references and commitments, not every buyer's identity.
- **Voice:** formal, cautious distinctions. “I can give you a number; I can't give you a face I never saw.” A favour is a defined obligation, not a repeatable grind. Can refuse to reveal a buyer he genuinely does not know.

### Keira Moss · auditor · 27–40
- **Silhouette:** practical coat and tool pouch; deliberate posture, fidgets when evidence is handled carelessly. Wants a safe, verifiable disclosure; fears innocent names being exposed by incomplete records. Holds the partial index and ability to explain its limitations.
- **Voice:** exact, corrects ambiguity, admits what she cannot prove. Arc moves from self-protection to a controlled disclosure when conditions warrant; alternate record access exists if she cannot personally help.

## 3. Main-campaign dialogue map and authored continuation

M01–M04 scripts and staged flow are in the companion slice document. M05–M11 descriptions, locations and checkpoints are in the campaign specification. Below are proposed critical dialogue exchanges to extend them; these are scene drafts, not yet the exhaustive branch scripts or timing sheets required for production.

### M05 · Sade's choice
**Trigger:** Ledger path committed; recipient knowledge and Sade trust determine opening. **Participants:** Marcus, Sade, optional Mina. **Changes:** location and who knows it.

**If Marcus promised anonymity and kept it:**  
**SADE:** You said you wouldn't put my name on it.  
**MARCUS:** I haven't. Someone saw the van near your stall.  
**SADE:** Then tell me what they actually know.  
**MARCUS:** A location. Not your name, as far as I can prove.

**If identity was shared:**  
**SADE:** You promised.  
**MARCUS:** I gave them your name. I thought it would get them to listen.  
**SADE:** You don't get to make that choice for me again.

**MINA:** There's a room behind my office. No one goes there unless my staff let them. Sade, you decide.  
**SADE:** And if I stay?  
**MINA:** We change who closes the stall and make sure you're not alone.

Player proposes Mina's room or support at the stall; Sade's response is determined by trust and her own safety assessment. Neither branch grants Mina the Ledger unless explicitly shared. If Mina is unavailable, a community escort through Ruth offers recovery.

### M06 · The sighting
**Trigger:** garage work order and Omar's identification. **Participants:** Marcus, Tessa, optionally Omar. **Changes:** Tessa trust and assistance.

**MARCUS:** You recognised that van.  
**TESSA:** The plate. Not the men inside.  
**MARCUS:** You watched me chase the wrong lead.  
**TESSA:** I was trying to keep them away from my employee's family. I should have told you.  
**MARCUS [listen]:** Tell me everything you remember. Start with the time.  
**TESSA:** Before Eli called. They waited across the street, then left toward Low Cross.  
**MARCUS [accuse]:** How am I supposed to trust you with anything now?  
**TESSA:** You don't have to. But the work order is real, and you can ask Omar.  
**OMAR, if present:** I repaired that corner. I can match the part to the order.

Both choices deliver the core lead; public accusation can lower trust and close the optional garage vehicle. The route via public transport remains.

### M07 · A signature is not an order
**Trigger:** authorisation comparison. **Participants:** Marcus, Keira, Celia; Voss only if contacted.

**KEIRA:** Eli signed the correction. The instruction next to it was added in a later revision.  
**MARCUS:** Can you prove who changed it?  
**KEIRA:** Not from this page. The server record can show which credential was used. A credential still isn't a person.  
**CELIA:** I contained the leak because I thought the company could investigate internally.  
**MARCUS:** You let a driver take the blame while you did it.  
**CELIA:** I knew he could be blamed. I chose the company. That choice is mine.

If EV-06 already exists, Keira may discuss its credential trace; otherwise she names it as a lead. The player can withhold the record from Celia; she then cannot later cite its contents.

### M08 · Lockdown
**Trigger:** records annex and EV-06 retrieval or terminal destruction. **Participants:** Marcus, Rusk by intercom, optional ally.

**RUSK:** You are in a restricted service area. Put down what you took and walk to the loading door.  
**MARCUS:** If you're trying to help, open the other exit.  
**RUSK:** That isn't available.  
**MARCUS:** Then neither is the record.

**If terminal preserved:** Keira confirms the copied record's source after receiving it. **If terminal destroyed:**  
**KEIRA:** I can request the certified off-site copy. It won't be immediate, and the request leaves a trail.  
**MARCUS:** Make the request. Don't put Sade's name on it.  
**KEIRA:** I don't need her name to request a server log.

Lockdown has two readable exits; optional allies offer help only when available. Loss triggers checkpoint retry, not a permanent state where the story cannot progress.

### M09 · Terms
**Trigger:** corroborating record secured or delayed copy received. **Participants:** Marcus, Ruth, Eli by call, chosen contact.

**RUTH:** You have a place and a time. Is that a plan?  
**MARCUS:** It's what I can confirm.  
**RUTH:** Then tell me what you can't.  
**MARCUS:** Who else will be there. Whether they'll let him go.

**ELI:** I kept the index because Omar was going to take the blame.  
**MARCUS:** You could have told me.  
**ELI:** You were leaving. I didn't want the last thing I gave you to be another reason to stay.  
**MARCUS:** You don't get to decide that for me.  
**ELI:** Then don't decide for Sade. Whatever you hand over, keep her name out of it.

Player selects whom to tell the exchange location. The interface lists each recipient and information shared. Eli's request is a tracked promise, not an automatically enforced action.

### M10 · Breakwater
**Trigger:** selected plan; entry varies with ally availability. **Participants:** Marcus, Eli, Rusk, Celia if present, other contacts only through established routes.

**RUSK:** The copy. Then your brother walks.  
**ELI:** Ask him about the original authorisation.  
**MARCUS:** A copy can disappear. The server record won't.  
**RUSK:** A credential isn't a conviction.  
**MARCUS:** No. But it's enough to start asking the right people.

**If corroborated record shared with Celia:**  
**CELIA:** Your authority to act for Meridian ends now, Victor. Security, stand down.  
**RUSK:** You think saying it out loud makes it true?

**If controlled disclosure:**  
**LENA, by call:** I have the copy you gave me. I haven't published it.  
**MARCUS:** Good. Hold it until Keira verifies the original.  
**LENA:** I will. That doesn't mean I can protect everyone you named.

The action that follows depends on the player's selected approach, not a forced universal firefight. Rusk can still contest the transfer; player has legible routes and a checkpoint. Eli moves when the path is clear and can call out a danger if he plausibly sees it.

### M11 · The cost
**Trigger:** successful escape. **Participants:** Marcus, Eli, Ruth, contacts conditionally. **Changes:** final handover, relationship and city aftermath.

**ELI:** You should know I signed the correction before I understood what it was for.  
**MARCUS:** I know. Keira showed me the revisions.  
**ELI:** I still signed it.  
**MARCUS:** Then we tell them that part too.

**RUTH:** Are you leaving now?  
**MARCUS:** I don't know.  
**RUTH:** That's the first honest answer you've given me in weeks.

**If Sade protected:** her stall reopens; she thanks the person who actually helped, without pretending risk vanished. **If promise broken:** she chooses distance and the stall may remain closed while she relocates; another witness may provide the necessary evidence. **If Tessa trusts Marcus:** she offers a ride; if not, she leaves the keys with Ruth and asks for space. The final handover identifies what evidence each recipient receives. Marcus's ringing phone echoes the opening; player may answer or leave it, after ending state is committed.
