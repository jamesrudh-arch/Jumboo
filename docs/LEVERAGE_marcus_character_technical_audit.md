# Marcus: character production research and audit

Status: **work in progress**. Owner approved the appearance direction, not these deformation tests or a production-ready character. This audit examines the separate experimental `marcus_neck_test.blend`; it is not the game and is not a replacement for the approved appearance scene.

## Verified baseline

- The scene uses one 53-bone skeleton with six separately skinned meshes. `tools/audit_marcus_rig.py` reports **68,022 triangles** before glTF export, with the head accounting for 38,752. Six meshes have one material slot each. This is a count, not a phone budget or a benchmark; final GPU vertices/materials and device costs may differ. Blender notes glTF can split vertices at UV and flat-shading discontinuities ([Blender glTF manual](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/4.2/addons/import_export/scene_gltf2.html)).
- At rest, all six meshes have a deform weight sum within 0.0001 of 1, zero unweighted vertices and at most four non-zero deform influences. These numbers do **not** prove that the chosen weights or joint centers produce good deformation. The head's weights were generated by a simple height approximation and have not been manually calibrated. [Blender's weight tools](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/4.2/sculpt_paint/weight_paint/editing.html) describe normalization; normalization is necessary hygiene, not visual validation.
- The audit counts 2,940 head boundary edges and 1,930 body boundary edges. Garment boundaries are normal for open clothing surfaces; even head/body boundaries can include deliberate cuts, loose components and UV-related seams, so these counts alone do not prove the number or location of defects. They do show that the composite must not be described as a closed, welded skin. The visible rear neck seam and unnatural side profile are separately confirmed by the renders.
- The separate `marcus_deformation_audit.blend` poses and `stress_{rest,arms,legs,head}.png` are **diagnostic**, not a walk, animation approval, or proof of collision-free clothing. In the sampled leg pose both feet float: the test does not use ground-contact controls. The elbow/shoulder and neck need closer side/rear and larger-range examination. No successful full movement/cloth-clipping audit has been completed.
- The tested appearance has no facial expression rig. Separate armature consolidation was checked at rest; transferring motion between different rigs still requires matching rest transforms as well as names, per [Godot 4.4 retargeting documentation](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.4/tutorials/assets_pipeline/retargeting_3d_skeletons.html).

## What the research changes

1. **Rebuild the join, not conceal it.** Blender's [Bridge Edge Loops](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/4.2/modeling/meshes/editing/edge/bridge_edge_loops.html) connects actual loops, including uneven loops. The present neck extension is not connected to the body; producing two clean anatomical neck boundaries, joining them into a continuous mesh with corresponding UVs/normals, and checking the joined area in multiple posed views is necessary. The head and body come from different sources with different meshes, proportions and texture layouts; bridging is not an automatic guarantee of a seamless result. Preserve the approved face and render before/after.
2. **Re-skin, then validate deformation.** [Data Transfer](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/4.2/modeling/modifiers/modify/data_transfer.html) can interpolate vertex groups and normals from a reference mesh but requires suitable mapping and destination data layers. Transfer near the junction from a correctly aligned body as a starting point; manually refine/normalize weights and check head pitch, yaw, shoulder raise and extreme combinations. Ensure the underlying joint locations fit the morphed body. A numerical weight sum is not proof of correct anatomy.
3. **Use garment-aware body masking and stress tests.** The pinned [CharacterCreator source](https://github.com/kblood/CharacterCreator/tree/852e48f316b90a0937234886630e4fb4cdfd1f78) describes fitted clothing, body-zone masking, joint adjustment and known prototype clipping. It does not establish that this assembled outfit is collision-free. Check jacket/shirt, cuff/hand, jeans/boot and hip/knee intersections under controlled poses, both front and back; remove only genuinely hidden body surfaces, not visible skin. Establish exact asset-specific regression cases rather than assuming the source's metrics apply here.
4. **Build grounded movement after deformation.** The existing swinging-leg test has no planted contact. A walk must be reviewed with contact, down, passing and up poses, foot placement through stance, pelvis/upper-body balance and loop continuity; see [GarageFarm's walk-cycle reference](https://garagefarm.net/blog/walk-cycle-easy-steps-to-animate-walking-animation-for-beginners). Foot sliding and hovering must be examined in both stationary/in-place and moving-world context. This is not yet done.
5. **Export and profile separately.** Blender's [glTF exporter](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/4.2/addons/import_export/scene_gltf2.html) supports skinned animation and specific material node patterns; the tinted node graph and packed textures must be checked after export, not assumed identical. Godot 4.4 needs correct rest transforms and bone mappings to share animations. [Godot's performance guide](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.4/tutorials/performance/optimizing_3d_performance.html) describes transparency and LOD trade-offs; actual iPhone performance requires on-device measurements. No Marcus GLB, Godot/Xogot import, material comparison, performance measurement or phone test has been performed.

## Reproducibility and limits

`tools/audit_marcus_rig.py` produces `docs/previews/marcus/rig_structural_audit.json`; `tools/build_marcus_deformation_audit.py` regenerates the posed Blender scene and images. Tested using Blender bpy 4.2 CPU Cycles with temporary headless display-library stubs; this does not verify Blender GUI or GPU. The counts describe this specific experimental scene, not a production asset or objective score. Images are rendered 3D model inspections, not runtime screenshots.
