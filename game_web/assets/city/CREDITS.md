# Third-party assets

Two CC0 kits. Both are vendored into this directory, so the game never depends
on a third-party host at runtime, and both licences are stored next to the files.

## Street kit: KayKit City Builder Bits 1.0

- **Author:** Kay Lousberg (KayKit)
- **Source:** https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-City-Builder-Bits-1.0
- **Licence:** CC0 1.0 Universal. See `../kit/LICENSE-KayKit-City-Builder-Bits.txt`.
  The pack's own licence file grants use in personal, educational and commercial
  projects with no attribution required. Attribution here is a courtesy.
- **Vendored:** 26 of the pack's 41 models, plus the shared 1024² texture atlas.
- **Used for:** cars, streetlights, traffic lights, hydrants, bins, benches,
  planters, utility boxes, awnings.
- **Audit:** every vendored model validated against the glTF 2.0 specification
  with its real atlas supplied (`tools/validate-kit.mjs`): 0 errors, 0 warnings.
  Whole kit is 26,813 triangles and one texture.

## City kit: Low Poly City Kit

- **Author:** kkyqr
- **Source:** https://github.com/kayquer/kit-cidade-lowpoly
- **Licence:** CC0 1.0 Universal. See `LICENSE-City-Kit-CC0.txt`.
- **Vendored:** 20 of the pack's 129 models (trees, park landscaping, street
  furniture used by parks).
- **Used for:** the trees along every street and in both parks, and the furniture
  that dresses the park lawns.
- **Audit:** all 129 models parse and were measured (129 models, 87,852
  triangles, 5.6 MB); the 20 vendored models are 848 KB. They use a palette of
  solid PBR colours rather than textures, so the trees instance cheaply.

## What is deliberately *not* third-party

The district's buildings, its road and bridge geometry, the debris, the
landmarks and the character rig are generated by this project. The kits above
are street furniture, vehicles, trees and park dressing. Generated towers carry
lit windows, rooftop tanks and aerials that the low-poly kits do not model, and
the city's scale (a 79 m tower) is well past what a 4 m building kit intends.
